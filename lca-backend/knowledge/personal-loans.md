# Personal loans

Sample policy document. Replace it with your own bank's personal loan policy; the amounts, rates and timings below are placeholders. In each section, the "Say:" line is what the agent can read to the customer, and the other sentences are the steps the agent takes.

## Personal loan policy overview

We offer flexible, collateral-free personal loans from 5,000 up to 500,000, with convenient repayment terms from 12 to 60 months in fixed monthly instalments. The interest rate remains fixed for the entire term based on the customer's credit profile and income. A nominal one-time processing fee of 1% applies upon disbursement, and approved funds are credited within 2 business days.

Say: "I would be delighted to assist you with our personal loan options. We offer convenient, collateral-free financing from 5,000 up to 500,000 with flexible terms to suit your needs. May I guide you through the details?"

## Eligibility

Applicants between 21 and 60 years of age with a regular monthly salary or business income of 20,000 or above are welcome to apply. Salaried applicants need at least 1 year in their current employment, and self-employed applicants need 2 years in business. A credit score of 700 or above qualifies for competitive rates. Existing commitments should remain within 50% of monthly income.

Say: "Certainly! We have straightforward eligibility criteria, including an age between 21 and 60 and a monthly income from 20,000. I would be pleased to review these details with you."

## Documents required

Application documentation is minimal and simple: proof of identity and proof of address. Salaried applicants provide their last 3 payslips and 6 months of bank statements, while self-employed applicants provide 2 years of tax returns and 6 months of statements. Documents can be uploaded seamlessly via our mobile app or submitted at any branch.

Say: "Applying is quick and convenient: you only need standard identity and income documents, which you can easily upload directly through our secure mobile application."

## How to apply

The application process is smooth and digital. Verify eligibility, explain the rate and terms clearly, and initiate the request through the mobile app under Loans & Personal Loan or directly on behalf of the customer. A quick identity verification is completed via OTP, followed by prompt credit assessment, with decisions typically provided within 24 hours.

Say: "I would be delighted to assist you with starting your application today. Our process is seamless, and you will typically receive a decision within 24 hours."

## Repayment and EMI

Repayments are effortlessly managed through automated monthly debits on a fixed date of the customer's choice, with complete visibility of the repayment schedule available anytime in the mobile app.

Say: "We offer hassle-free automated monthly instalments with fixed amounts throughout the term, and you can conveniently track your full schedule anytime via our app."

## Early repayment

Customers enjoy the flexibility to make partial prepayments or settle the loan in full after 6 months. For full settlements, a standard 2% fee applies to the outstanding balance, with one complimentary partial prepayment permitted each year.

Say: "You have the flexibility to make early repayments after 6 months, and I would be pleased to calculate the exact figure and options whenever you wish."

## Missed or late payments

We are committed to helping customers stay on track. If unexpected financial challenges arise, proactive guidance is provided, and support solutions can be arranged to assist the customer.

Say: "We are always here to support you through unexpected situations. I can connect you with our dedicated support team to arrange a comfortable solution for you."
