# Cards and ATM

Sample policy document. Replace it with your own bank's card policy. In each section, the "Say:" line is what the agent can read to the customer, and the other sentences are the steps the agent takes.

## Lost or stolen card

Block the card straight away, before anything else. Verify the customer's identity with two of: date of birth, registered mobile number, last transaction amount. Ask if there are any transactions the customer doesn't recognise and raise a fraud dispute for each one. Order a replacement card: it arrives in 5 to 7 business days, or in 2 business days with express delivery. The customer can keep using mobile banking and the virtual card in the app until the new card arrives.

Say: "I'm sorry to hear that. I'll block your card right now so nobody can use it, and then I'll order a replacement for you."

## ATM did not dispense cash

If the ATM took the money from the account but no cash came out, raise an ATM dispute with the ATM location, date, time and amount. Failed ATM withdrawals are normally reversed automatically within 5 business days. If the money is not back after 5 business days, the dispute is escalated and the customer is credited within 10 business days.

Say: "I understand how worrying that is. I've raised a dispute for this withdrawal, and the amount usually comes back to your account within 5 business days."

## Card swallowed by an ATM

Block the card, because it cannot be returned to the customer for security reasons. Order a replacement card, which arrives in 5 to 7 business days. If the ATM belongs to another bank, tell the customer not to contact that bank about the card.

Say: "For your security I'll block that card now and send you a new one; it should reach you in 5 to 7 business days."

## Forgotten or blocked PIN

The PIN is blocked after 3 wrong attempts. The customer can set a new PIN in the mobile app under Cards, then Reset PIN, after confirming a one-time code. Never ask for or read out the customer's PIN.

Say: "You can set a new PIN yourself in the app under Cards, then Reset PIN. It takes about a minute and works straight away."

## Card declined

Check the card status first: blocked, expired, or over its daily limit. Daily limits are 1,000 for ATM withdrawals and 5,000 for card payments, and the customer can raise them in the app. If the card was blocked by fraud checks, confirm the recent transactions with the customer and unblock it.

Say: "Let me check what happened with that payment. I can see the reason on my screen and I'll get it sorted for you now."

## New card not received

New and replacement cards arrive in 5 to 7 business days. If 10 business days have passed, block the card in the post and order another one with express delivery at no charge.

Say: "I'm sorry your card hasn't arrived. I'll cancel the one in the post and send a new one by express delivery, free of charge."
