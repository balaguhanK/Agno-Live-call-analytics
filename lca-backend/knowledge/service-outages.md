# Service outages

Sample policy document. Replace it with your own.

## Reporting an outage

Check the outage map for the customer's area first. If no outage is listed, run a line test and open a fault ticket with the network team. Give the customer the ticket number.

## Compensation for repeated outages

Customers with three or more outages in 30 days are eligible for a credit of one month's service fee. Apply the credit to the next bill and note the ticket numbers on the account.
