# Cancellations and retention

Sample policy document. Replace it with your own.

## Before cancelling

Ask what prompted the cancellation. If it is price, offer the loyalty discount of 20 percent for 12 months. If it is service quality, check for open fault tickets and offer to escalate.

## Processing a cancellation

Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing cycle. There is no cancellation fee after the minimum 12-month term. Send the customer a written confirmation with their final bill date.

## Escalation to a supervisor

If the customer asks for a manager, offer a supervisor callback within 2 hours or transfer to the supervisor queue if one is available.
