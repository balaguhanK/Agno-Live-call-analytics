# Account access

Sample policy document. Replace it with your own.

## Password reset

Customers can reset their password from the sign-in page using "Forgot password". The reset link is sent to the email address on the account and expires after 30 minutes. Never read a password out loud or ask the customer for their current password.

## Locked accounts

After 5 failed sign-in attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes. Agents can unlock it sooner after verifying the customer's identity with two of: date of birth, billing postcode, last payment amount.

## Changing the account email

Verify the customer's identity first, then update the email. A confirmation is sent to both the old and the new address.
