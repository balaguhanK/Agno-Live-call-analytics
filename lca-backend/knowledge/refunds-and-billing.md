# Refunds and billing

Sample policy document. Replace it with your own; every .md or .txt file in this folder is searchable by the Agent Assist Bot.

## Duplicate or incorrect charges

If a customer was charged twice for the same service, refund the duplicate charge to the original payment method. Refunds appear on the customer's card within 3 to 5 business days. Confirm the charge date and amount with the customer before issuing the refund.

## Billing date changes

Customers can move their monthly billing date once every 6 months. The change takes effect from the next billing cycle and the first bill after the change is prorated.

## Disputed charges

If the customer disputes a charge you can't verify, open a billing investigation. The billing team replies within 2 business days, and late fees are paused while the investigation is open.
